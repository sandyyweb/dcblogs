How much does interior design cost in Bangalore?

A 2BHK full-home interior in Bangalore typically costs roughly ₹6–12 lakh and a 3BHK ₹10–20 lakh, depending on carpentry scope, material grade and finish. An itemised quote is the most reliable way to compare studios.

How long does a full-home interior project take?

Most full-home projects take around 8 to 14 weeks across design sign-off, factory production and on-site installation. Scope changes and late approvals are the most common causes of delay.

Which plywood is best for Bangalore’s humid weather?

BWR or BWP grade plywood with a moisture-resistant finish suits Bangalore’s monsoon humidity. Marine ply is the safest choice for kitchens and wet areas.
