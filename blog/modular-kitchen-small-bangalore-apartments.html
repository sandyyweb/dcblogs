Best Modular Kitchen Layouts for Small Apartments in Bangalore

By Dezign Code studio, HSR Layout9 Nov 20265 min read
The short answerIn a compact flat, an L-shaped kitchen is the safest default, a parallel kitchen suits narrow rooms, and a U-shape only works if the room is wide enough to keep a clear walkway between the runs.
In this article
  1. L-shaped modular kitchen: the flexible default
  2. Parallel modular kitchen: best for narrow rooms
  3. Modular kitchen storage and ventilation tips
  4. Modular kitchen FAQs

L-shaped modular kitchen: the flexible default

Two adjoining counters keep the sink, hob and fridge within easy reach, and leave one corner for a small table or breakfast counter. Corner units need carousel or pull-out hardware to be usable.

Parallel modular kitchen: best for narrow rooms

Two facing counters make the most of a long, thin space. Keep the walkway wide enough for two people to pass, and put the hob and sink on opposite runs.

Modular kitchen storage and ventilation tips

Tall units, loft cabinets and deep drawers add capacity without adding floor area. Position the chimney and window so cooking smoke has a clear path out of the flat.

Modular kitchen FAQs

Is a U-shaped kitchen possible in a 2BHK?

Only if the kitchen is wide enough for a comfortable walkway. Otherwise an L-shape with a tall unit is a better fit.

Which finish is easiest to maintain?

Laminate and acrylic wipe clean easily. Ask for edge banding and sealing near the sink.
