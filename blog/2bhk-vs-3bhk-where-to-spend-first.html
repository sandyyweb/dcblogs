2BHK & 3BHK Interior Design Budget: Where to Spend First

By Dezign Code studio, HSR Layout26 Oct 20264 min read
The short answerSpend first on what is hard to change later: the kitchen, built-in storage and electrical and lighting points. Loose furniture and décor can follow once you have lived in the space for a few weeks.
In this article
  1. Spend first on the modular kitchen and storage
  2. Plan electrical points and lighting early
  3. Leave living room décor and loose furniture for last
  4. 2BHK and 3BHK interior budget FAQs

Spend first on the modular kitchen and storage

These are fixed, daily-use items that are expensive to redo. Good hardware and planned storage pay back every day.

Plan electrical points and lighting early

Points for lights, fans, appliances and TV are cheapest to place before the walls are finished. Layered lighting changes a room more than most furniture does.

Leave living room décor and loose furniture for last

Sofas, rugs and décor are easy to add, swap or upgrade. Waiting also tells you what the room actually needs.

2BHK and 3BHK interior budget FAQs

Should the master bedroom come first?

Do the wardrobes first, since they are built-in, and add the rest over time.

How much should I keep for contingency?

Many homeowners keep a small reserve for site surprises. Ask your designer what is realistic for your scope.
