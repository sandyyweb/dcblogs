Best Plywood for Bangalore’s Humid Weather: BWR, BWP & Marine Ply Compared

By Dezign Code studio, HSR Layout2 Nov 20264 min read
The short answerUse BWP or marine-grade ply for kitchens and bathrooms, where moisture is constant, and BWR or good commercial ply for bedroom wardrobes and living areas. The finish and edge sealing matter as much as the core.

BWR, BWP, commercial and marine plywood explained

Commercial ply is meant for dry interiors. BWR resists moisture for a time. BWP is built for repeated wetting, and marine ply is the top tier for constant damp.

Which plywood grade to use in each room

Kitchen base units and anything near plumbing should be water-proof grade. Wardrobes, shelves and TV units in dry rooms rarely need to be.

Best finishes and edge sealing for humid weather

A laminate or acrylic skin protects the face, but swelling usually starts at unsealed edges. Ask how edges are banded and sealed, and for the brand and thickness in writing.

Plywood and finish FAQs

Is marine ply worth it everywhere?

Usually not. Use it where moisture is constant and use a lower grade elsewhere to save budget.

How do I check the grade?

Ask for the brand, grade and thickness in the quote, and check the stamp on the board during delivery.
