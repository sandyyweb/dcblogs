How Long Does a Full-Home Interior Project Take in Bangalore?

By Dezign Code studio, HSR Layout16 Nov 20264 min read
The short answerMost full-home projects run in three stages: design and sign-off, factory production, then site installation. Plan on roughly 10 to 14 weeks from first drawings to handover, and expect the design stage to be the one you control most.
In this article
  1. Design sign-off: the first stage of a home interior project
  2. Factory production time for modular interiors
  3. On-site installation and handover time
  4. Interior project timeline FAQs

Design sign-off: the first stage of a home interior project

This is where the schedule is won or lost. Measurements, layouts, material selections and electrical points all need to be final before production starts. Every change after sign-off pushes the dates.

Factory production time for modular interiors

Carpentry units are cut and finished off-site while the flat is prepared. Lead time depends on the finish: laminate is quick, while veneer, PU and imported hardware take longer.

On-site installation and handover time

Civil and electrical work happens first, then installation, then painting touch-ups and a final clean. A snag list walk-through before handover keeps small fixes from lingering.

Interior project timeline FAQs

Can I live in the flat during the work?

It is possible for a partial scope, but dust and noise make it hard. An empty flat is faster and cleaner.

What delays projects most?

Late design changes and materials chosen after production has begun.
